🌊 **RIVER WATCH APP**  
**Monitor • Report • Map: Community Action for Clean Water**

---

### 🌿 A Cleaner, More Beautiful Vision

River Watch is a **lightweight, mobile-first web application** designed for real community impact along the Hillsborough River corridor. It turns everyday citizens into environmental stewards by making water-quality monitoring and pollution reporting simple, visual, and collaborative.

No heavy frameworks. No forced logins. Just pure, fast, beautiful tools that put the river’s health in the hands of the people who live beside it.

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack (Refined & Polished)

| Layer              | Technology                                      | Why It Feels Good |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Frontend**       | Pure HTML5 + CSS3 + Vanilla JS (ES6+)           | Lightning-fast, zero bloat |
| **Styling**        | Custom CSS variables, CSS Grid & Flexbox, Google Fonts (DM Sans + Fraunces) | Soft, modern, highly readable typography |
| **Mapping**        | Leaflet.js v1.9.4                               | Smooth interactive maps with custom markers |
| **Backend**        | Supabase JS Client v2.49.1                      | Real-time data + anonymous upvoting without accounts |

Everything is intentionally minimal so the experience stays fluid on any phone, even on slower connections.

---

### ✨ Core Features (Elevated)

**🗺️ Interactive Map**  
See official USGS & EPC monitoring stations side-by-side with live community reports. Custom markers for algae blooms, oil spills, trash, and more float elegantly over the river.

**📝 Community Incident Reports**  
Drop a geotagged report in seconds. Choose a category, add a photo, and share what you see. Every report becomes part of a living map of the river’s health.

**📊 Field Sensor Logging**  
Log real measurements right from the riverbank:  
- pH  
- Turbidity (NTU)  
- Dissolved Oxygen (mg/L)  
- Water temperature  

Your readings appear instantly on the map as clean, readable data points.

**👍 Upvoting & Tracking**  
Support reports that matter with a single tap. Device-level identification keeps everything anonymous while still letting the community surface the most urgent issues.

**📚 Educational Hub**  
Beautifully written, short articles that explain what the numbers actually mean — pH thresholds, dissolved oxygen levels, turbidity ranges — so anyone can understand the science without feeling overwhelmed.

---

### 💚 Design Philosophy

The app should feel as calm and clear as the water we want to protect. Soft gradients, generous spacing, thoughtful typography, and a river-inspired color palette create an experience that is both useful and quietly beautiful.

This is community science, made elegant.
